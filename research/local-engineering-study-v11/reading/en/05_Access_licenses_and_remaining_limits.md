The earlier deposit preserves deployment-only PPL reports and the pinned WikiText reconstruction recipe. It does not supply new PPL results for the two v11 artifacts. No independent human technical review or second hardware run is completed. Offline and same-machine reference checks cannot replace these. The study remains a finite, localized five-defect experiment; broad engineering reliability and Nature editorial acceptance are not established.
